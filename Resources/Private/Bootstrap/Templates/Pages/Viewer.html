Page template of the viewer of the Kitodo.Presentation demo site sets, with a sidebar on the left. For newspapers and years (isCalendar), the calendar is shown instead of the main content (toolbox, page view, ...). For audio, video and 3D objects (mediaType), the media player or 3D viewer is shown instead of the main content. The content of the slot "pages" (e.g. the page grid) is shown in a sidebar on the right, except for newspapers, years and media, which have no page images.
